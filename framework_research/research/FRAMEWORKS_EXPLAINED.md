# Investment Frameworks - Detailed Explanations

## Overview

9 different investment frameworks to test on Indian market (NSE). Each represents a distinct philosophy about what makes a good stock.

---

## 1️⃣ PURE FUNDAMENTALS
**Philosophy:** Classic balanced approach

**Scoring:**
- Quality (ROE): 40%
- Valuation (PE Ratio): 35%
- Safety (Debt-to-Equity): 25%

**Ideal Stock:**
- ROE > 20% (excellent profitability)
- PE < 20 (reasonable valuation)
- Debt/Equity < 1.0 (manageable debt)

**Example:** TCS or Infosys when trading at reasonable multiples

**When it wins:**
- Stable market conditions
- Companies with consistent earnings

**When it struggles:**
- Market overvalues growth stories
- Sudden profit margin compression

---

## 2️⃣ GROWTH AT VALUE
**Philosophy:** High growth at reasonable price (best of both worlds)

**Scoring:**
- Revenue CAGR: 45%
- PEG Ratio (PE/Growth): 30%
- ROE Quality: 25%

**Ideal Stock:**
- Revenue CAGR > 15%
- PEG Ratio < 1.5 (not expensive relative to growth)
- ROE > 15% (profitable growth)

**Example:** Auto suppliers, IT services, fintech in growth phase

**When it wins:**
- Rising interest rates (growth premium compresses)
- Companies near inflection point
- Earnings acceleration beginning

**When it struggles:**
- Bull markets (everything grows at any price)
- Slowdown in growth trajectory

---

## 3️⃣ QUALITY FOCUS
**Philosophy:** Exceptional businesses at any reasonable price (Buffett-style)

**Scoring:**
- ROE: 45%
- Asset Turnover (Efficiency): 25%
- Low Debt: 30%

**Ideal Stock:**
- ROE > 25% (exceptional)
- Asset Turnover > 1.5x (very efficient)
- Debt/Equity < 0.3 (fortress balance sheet)

**Example:** HDFC Bank, Asian Paints, Nestle

**When it wins:**
- Long holding periods (quality compounds)
- Risk-off markets (quality outperforms)
- Dividend focus

**When it struggles:**
- Value stocks massively undervalued
- Cheap garbage beats expensive quality

---

## 4️⃣ DIVIDEND YIELD
**Philosophy:** Income-focused investing

**Scoring:**
- Dividend Yield: 40%
- Payout Ratio Sustainability: 35%
- Quality (ROE): 25%

**Ideal Stock:**
- Dividend Yield > 3%
- Payout Ratio 30-60% (sustainable, not cutting)
- ROE > 15% (profitable enough to sustain dividend)

**Example:** SBIN, PSU banks, telecom, utilities

**When it wins:**
- Rising interest rate environment (yield attractive)
- Retiree/income-focused portfolios
- Bear markets (steady dividends help)

**When it struggles:**
- Bull markets (capital gains beat dividends)
- Dividend cuts (sudden payout ratio increase)

---

## 5️⃣ CLASSIC VALUE
**Philosophy:** Deep value: exceptional quality at distressed prices (pure value play)

**Scoring:**
- Valuation Bargain (Low PE): 35%
- Quality (High ROE): 35%
- Safety (Low Debt + High CR): 30%

**Ideal Stock:**
- PE < 12 (extremely cheap)
- ROE > 20% (excellent quality)
- Debt/Equity < 0.5 (safe)
- Trading below book value (bonus)

**Example:** Tempsens Instruments (from your screenshot), overlooked PSU banks

**When it wins:**
- Market crash (stocks become cheap)
- Sector rotation out (sector stocks beaten down)
- Turnarounds near completion

**When it struggles:**
- Bull markets (expensive stocks stay expensive)
- Value traps (cheap for a reason)

---

## 6️⃣ FREE CASH FLOW
**Philosophy:** Cash is king (only real earnings count)

**Scoring:**
- FCF Yield (FCF/Market Cap): 40%
- OCF/Net Income Quality: 30%
- CapEx Efficiency: 30%

**Ideal Stock:**
- Free Cash Flow Yield > 5% (lots of real cash)
- OCF/NI > 1.0 (earnings are real, not accounting)
- CapEx/Revenue < 3% (asset-light business)

**Example:** IT services (asset-light), Financial services (high FCF)

**When it wins:**
- Credit crunches (only cash matters)
- Financial engineering exposed (accounting vs reality)
- Capital allocation scrutiny

**When it struggles:**
- Growth investments (capex-heavy but FCF negative)
- Turnarounds (negative or low FCF initially)

---

## 7️⃣ SMALL CAP GEM
**Philosophy:** Find quality small caps before they scale up

**Scoring:**
- Quality (High ROE): 40%
- Growth (Revenue CAGR): 35%
- Safety (Low Debt): 25%

**Ideal Stock:**
- Market Cap ₹500Cr - ₹5000Cr (true small cap)
- ROE > 20% (stand out in small cap land)
- Revenue CAGR > 15% (growing rapidly)
- Debt/Equity < 0.7 (safe)

**Example:** Emerging tech companies, specialty chemicals, niche manufacturers

**When it wins:**
- Small cap outperformance cycle
- Pre-IPO/listing growth stage
- Sector consolidation (clear winners emerge)

**When it struggles:**
- Market correction (small caps hardest hit)
- Growth slowdown (valuation compression)
- Liquidity dries up (hard to exit)

---

## 8️⃣ MOMENTUM
**Philosophy:** Trend is your friend (technical + relative strength)

**Scoring:**
- 52-Week Price Momentum: 40%
- Relative Strength vs Index: 35%
- Volume Trend: 25%

**Ideal Stock:**
- 52-Week Return > 30% (strong trend)
- Relative Strength > 1.1 (outperforming index)
- Volume Increasing (buyer conviction)
- RSI 30-70 (healthy momentum zone)

**Example:** Sector leaders during sector rotation, breakout stocks

**When it wins:**
- Trending markets (up or down)
- Sector rotation plays
- Mid-trend entry points

**When it struggles:**
- Reversals (momentum reverses suddenly)
- Overbought tops (crashes from highs)
- Mean reversion (expensive gets cheap, cheap gets expensive)

---

## 9️⃣ DEFENSIVE BLUE CHIP
**Philosophy:** Boring, stable, sleep-well-at-night stocks

**Scoring:**
- Dividend Yield: 30%
- Earnings Stability (Low Beta): 25%
- Moat/Quality (High ROE): 25%
- Balance Sheet Strength: 20%

**Ideal Stock:**
- Dividend Yield > 2.5% (income)
- Beta < 0.9 (less volatile than market)
- Debt/Equity < 0.5 (safe)
- ROE > 15% (has competitive moat)

**Example:** HDFC Bank, ITC, Bajaj Auto (defensive sectors)

**When it wins:**
- Bear markets (stable outperforms volatile)
- High inflation (passed to customers via pricing power)
- Rising interest rates (safety premium)

**When it struggles:**
- Bull markets (growth outperforms safety)
- Disintermediation (moat threatened)
- Margin compression (cost inflation)

## 🎯 Framework Selection Guide

**Choose based on your personality:**

### Conservative (Low Risk)
→ **Defensive Blue Chip** or **Quality Focus**
- Sleep better at night
- Lower volatility
- Steady dividends

### Balanced
→ **Pure Fundamentals** or **Free Cash Flow**
- Good balance of growth + safety
- Reasonable valuations
- Sustainable businesses

### Growth-Oriented
→ **Growth at Value** or **Small Cap Gem**
- Higher potential returns
- Still reasonable valuations
- Growing companies

### Aggressive/Value
→ **Classic Value** or **Momentum**
- Highest potential returns
- Higher risk/volatility
- Specialized market conditions

### Contrarian
→ **Momentum** (opposite signal) or **Classic Value**
- Buy when others fear
- High conviction needed
- Timing critical

## 📈 Testing Methodology

**Phase 1: Snapshot (Month 1)**
```
Day 1: Capture market fundamentals
  → Save all stock data to data/snapshots/
  → Record: PE, ROE, Debt, Yield, etc.
```

**Phase 2: Holding (Months 1-12)**
```
No action. Market does its thing.
Stocks go up, down, sideways.
Frameworks stay frozen in time.
```

**Phase 3: Backtest (Month 13)**
```
Load snapshot from Month 1
Score all stocks with each framework
Rank top 5 per framework
Measure: Did top 5 outperform market?
  → Average return %
  → Win rate (how many up?)
  → Best/worst performer
  → Sharpe ratio (risk-adjusted)
```

**Phase 4: Report**
```
Generate comparison report
Which framework won?
Why did it work?
Plan improvements
```

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. **No Framework Always Wins**
Different frameworks win in different market conditions:
- **Value wins** in crashes
- **Growth wins** in bull markets
- **Momentum wins** in trending markets
- **Quality wins** in uncertainty

### 2. **Combining Frameworks is Powerful**
Instead of picking one framework, **vote with all 9**:
```
Stock Score = Average of all framework scores
Only pick stocks scoring > 6.0 on average
This reduces false signals
```

### 3. **Framework Signals Weakest Near Peaks**
Best framework signals are **contrarian**:
- Value highest when most unloved (crashes)
- Momentum highest when most crowded (tops)
- Quality highest when most feared (recessions)

### 4. **Sector Matters As Much As Framework**
Run frameworks **per sector**:
- IT sector may favor "Growth at Value"
- Banks may favor "Dividend Yield"
- Pharma may favor "Quality Focus"

### 5. **Market Phase Matters**
Track when each framework outperforms:
- Bull market: Growth + Momentum
- Bear market: Quality + Defensive
- Volatility: Classic Value
- Transition: Momentum
